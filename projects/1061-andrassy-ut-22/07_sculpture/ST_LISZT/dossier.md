# Liszt Ferenc — seated statue (ST_LISZT)

- Subject: Liszt Ferenc (1811–1886), seated, in an abbé's cassock, book/score on the lap, right hand resting on it (S23 photo).
- Sculptor: Strobl Alajos; commissioned c.1882 (S14, S18 comment); limestone (S14).
- Position: niche right of the main entrance as seen from Andrássy út (S19, single source → C); niche with shell
  half-dome, statue on a plain rectangular plinth (S23).
- Liszt and Erkel reportedly sat for the sculptor themselves (search summary of S14; verify in Szvoboda 2007).
- History: head shot off in WWII and restored (S14). Today: restored figure in place (not independently dated).
- Size: seated figure ≈ 2.6 m incl. plinth top (model C; estimated from niche height on S23 vs niche 4.2 m).
- 3D: placeholder figure in the viewer (D). A 2.5D relief needs a clean cut-out of S23 (background is the niche
  stone → manual masking or a background-removal tool); not made, to avoid presenting a guess.
