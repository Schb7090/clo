# Melpomene — muse of tragedy (MU_MELPO)

- Standing figure, crowned (mural/diadem), dagger in the lowered right hand, drapery with girdle; stands on an
  ornate pedestal with a theatre mask relief and garland; shell-headed niche (S23).
- Sculptor: "Brestyanszky Albert, szobrász" on the 1883 plate (S23); S14/S15 give Brestyánszky Béla → conflict logged.
- Position: niche on a side façade of the front pavilion (S14/S15: Thalia and Melpomene on the side façades);
  which side is not documented → modelled on the Dalszínház u. side (D).
- Height: ≈ 3.0 m figure (C, from niche proportions).
