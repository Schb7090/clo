# Composer statues on the attic terraces (CO01–CO16 original, CN01–CN16 replacement)

## Originals (1881–1939)
- 16 figures, Sóskút limestone, placed September 1881; subjects chosen June 1880 by the committee chaired by
  Podmaniczky Frigyes (S14).
- Subjects and sculptors (S15 per-sculptor list): Guido d'Arezzo, Pergolesi, Lasso — Donáth Gyula; Palestrina, Peri —
  Kis(s) György; Weber, Rossini, Lully — Brestyánszky Béla; Gluck, Meyerbeer, Mozart — Szász Gyula; Cherubini,
  Spontini — Strobl Alajos; Beethoven, Haydn, Wagner — Huszár Adolf.
- Standing portrait figures in period costume, each on a balustrade pedestal in front of an attic pilaster (S10 detail).
- 19 Jan 1939: a ≈1.5 m statue fell; the rest were removed soon after (S14). The original order on the building
  is not documented → positions in the model are D.

## Replacements (Dec 1966 per S14; 1965 per S15)
- Left to right (S14): Monteverdi, Scarlatti, Gluck, Mozart, Beethoven, Rossini, Donizetti, Glinka, Wagner, Verdi,
  Gounod, Bizet, Musorgszkij, Csajkovszkij, Moniuszko, Smetana.
- Sculptors (S15): Ispánki József (Monteverdi, Smetana), Győry Dezső, Marton László, Tar István, Mikus Sándor,
  Pátzay Pál, Váradi Sándor, Antal Károly (two each).
- Stone: Bulgarian limestone, ≈25 q each (S14) vs Süttő limestone (S15).
